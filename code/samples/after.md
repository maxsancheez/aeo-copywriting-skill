# How long to steep cold brew (and how to store it)

Last updated: 2026-10-01 · Example page for a fictional brand, Northbean.

Steep cold brew for 12 to 18 hours in the fridge, using coarse-ground coffee at a 1:8 coffee-to-water ratio by weight. That gives you a ready-to-drink batch with no dilution needed.

## How long should cold brew steep?

Cold brew should steep for 12 to 18 hours in the fridge, or 10 to 12 hours at room temperature. Under 10 hours tastes thin and sour. Past 24 hours, the coffee picks up woody, bitter notes [1].

## What ratio of coffee to water should I use?

Use a 1:8 ratio by weight for ready-to-drink cold brew: 100 g of coffee to 800 g of water. Use 1:4 if you want a concentrate you'll cut with milk or water later. The Northbean Cold Brew Pitcher marks both fill lines on the glass.

## What grind size works for cold brew?

Cold brew needs a coarse grind, about the size of raw sugar. A fine grind over-extracts in a long steep and clogs the filter. On a Baratza Encore grinder, that's roughly setting 28 to 32.

## How long does cold brew last in the fridge?

Undiluted cold brew lasts about 7 to 10 days in a sealed jar in the fridge. Concentrate lasts about 2 weeks. Once you add milk, drink it within 24 hours.

## Cold brew vs. iced coffee

| | Cold brew | Iced coffee |
|---|---|---|
| Brew time | 12–18 hours | 4 minutes |
| Water temp | Cold | Hot, then chilled |
| Taste | Smooth, low acid | Brighter, more acidic |
