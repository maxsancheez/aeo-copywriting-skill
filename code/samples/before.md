# The Ultimate Guide to Cold Brew

In today's busy world, more and more people are discovering the joys of cold brew coffee, and it's easy to see why so many coffee lovers have made the switch from traditional iced coffee over the past few years.

## Why cold brew?

Have you ever wondered why cold brew tastes so smooth? There are a lot of reasons for this, and this tool makes it easier than ever. Our product is a game-changer for anyone who wants great coffee at home without spending a lot of money at coffee shops every single morning before work.

## How long should it steep?

This is one of the most common questions we get. As mentioned above, the process takes time, and various factors like grind size, temperature and the beans you use can all affect how long you should leave it, so it's really up to personal preference and experimentation in the end.

## Storing it

It keeps for a while in the fridge. Some people say it lasts longer than regular coffee, but it depends.
